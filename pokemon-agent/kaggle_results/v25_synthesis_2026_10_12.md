## Reading

1. **Deck lever re-confirmed on this branch's infra**: 35W-noBoss adds
   ~+1.5..1.75pp vs 30W in totals (K2), significant on Bill (+6.12pp, z=2.55).
   Magnitude below a450b00f's +2.48pp z=4.22 — their pools (meta1012, water)
   are 35W-heavier; direction and sign in every pool consistent.
2. **Knobs are regime-split, net zero**: −0.98pp vs v22b-policy opponents
   (self-play reference), +1.31pp vs thickbot, incl. **+2.25pp in the 35W
   mirror** — the exact live failure mode from forensics (61/62 losses =
   thin board vs thick 3-4 board, walls not retreating into 350 range).
   No pool in either config is significantly negative (worst: −2.50pp
   z=−1.01, −2.00pp z=−1.26 — both n.s.).
3. **Why still ship v25 (not plain v22b+35W)**: the live field is not v22b
   self-play (both branches' post-mortems: self-play gauntlet overestimates).
   Live = diverse thick boards (top30: 13× Ogerpon, 4× Lucario, stalls,
   new Metal/Duraludon archetypes). The knobs' +1.3pp live-like-regime edge
   plus the live 484 precedent outweigh their −1.0pp cost against a
   v22b-clone opponent that barely exists live (mirrors ≈ 15% of games).
   Honest caveat: every knob cell is |z|<1.6 — directional evidence, not
   proof. Pre-registered readout unchanged: our_replays WR at 24-48h
   (keep ≥~50%, revert <~45%).
